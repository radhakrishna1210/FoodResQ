# Posting surplus food
1. Open **Post surplus food** on your dashboard.
2. Fill in the food name, category, diet (Veg / Egg / Non-veg), servings, when it was prepared and how it is stored.
3. Choose a **Pickup by** time. FoodResQ also computes a conservative safe pickup deadline from the storage condition; the earlier of the two is shown as "Pickup by".
4. Add the pickup location, instructions, a contact phone and a photo. Posts without a photo need a quick review before matching.
5. Tick the five safety checklist items and the declaration yourself, then tap **Post and find a Receiver**.
