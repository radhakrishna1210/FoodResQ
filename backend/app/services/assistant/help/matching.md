# How matching works
When you post surplus food, our engine checks every verified Receiver and finds the best fit for it — not just the nearest one. It looks at things like distance, how much they can take, timing, and what food types and diets they accept, and rules out anyone who can't take it in time. The best match gets an offer first; if they can't accept, the next best Receiver is offered automatically, and the search area widens if needed. You'll always see plain-language reasons for every match.
