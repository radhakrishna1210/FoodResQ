# How JEV matching works
JEV is FoodResQ's decision engine. First it removes Receivers who can't take the food (not verified, unavailable, closed on arrival, different diet, food type not accepted, too far, can't arrive before the deadline, or no capacity). Then it scores the rest from 0 to 100 on distance, capacity, timing feasibility, today's demand, reliability, diet fit and opening hours. Urgent food gives more weight to distance and timing. The best Receiver(s) get an offer; if they decline or don't respond, the next best is offered, and the search area widens from 10 to 15 to 20 km.
